Sizing repeated bets

Lecture notes · Section 3.2

Suppose a gambler faces the same favourable bet many times: it wins with probability p, loses with probability q = 1 − p, and pays b to 1. Betting too little wastes the edge; betting too much risks ruin. How much of the bankroll should be wagered each round?

The answer depends on what is being optimized. Maximizing expected wealth says to bet everything every time, which goes broke with probability one. Kelly (1956) proposed a different target.

The resulting strategy grows wealth faster in the long run than any essentially different strategy, and never risks the whole bankroll.
