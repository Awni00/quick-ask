# Geometric icon concepts

## q-launch
- Idea: a "Q" (for Quick) built as an open ring that flings its tail off as a separate dot, so the Q's tail doubles as the dot of a "?".
- AI signal: the ring is a spinner/progress arc (faint full track behind, gradient brightening toward the head), the "thinking" indicator, and the glowing dot reads as the answer arriving.

## bolt-ask
- Idea: a "?" whose stem drops as one crisp lightning zig with sharp miter corners: a question, answered fast.
- AI signal: only the electric indigo-to-cyan gradient on a cool white body; the bolt suggests energy/compute rather than a mascot.

## orb-ask
- Idea: a clean monoline "?" whose dot is a luminous orb inside a thin halo ring.
- AI signal: the dot becomes a glowing, ringed "presence", a model waiting to answer, without using sparkles or a face.
