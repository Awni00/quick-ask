# Letterform concepts

## q-nest
- Idea: a stressed geometric Q with a swash tail, and a lowercase "a" set inside its counter: the answer lives inside the question (Q&A as one monogram).
- AI signal: the "a" is the only lit element, a cool teal-to-blue gradient with a faint glow, as if generated inside the Q. (Menu bar: the "a" reads as "@" at 18 px, so it collapses to a solid dot in the counter.)

## q-stream
- Idea: a Q whose tail breaks into a decelerating trail (bar, dash, dot): speed lines for "quick", and a Q you can read at a glance.
- AI signal: the trail shifts from ink to indigo to cyan and reads as tokens streaming out of the question.

## interrobang
- Idea: a hand-drawn interrobang (question and exclamation in one glyph), with the exclamation stem cutting a clean channel through the question hook: question and answer in a single mark.
- AI signal: the shared dot, where question and answer resolve, is the one luminous mint-to-cyan accent.
