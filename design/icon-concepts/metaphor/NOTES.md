# Metaphor / object concepts

## service-bell
- Idea: a chrome front-desk service bell. One tap and help appears, which matches the hotkey summoning an answer on demand ("ding").
- AI signal: the "ding" arcs and the faint glow on the dome crown use a cool cyan-to-violet gradient, the only non-physical element in an otherwise real object.

## oracle-ball
- Idea: a modern Magic 8-Ball. You ask a quick question and an answer floats up in the window; the die shows a "?" in the waiting state.
- AI signal: an oracle that predicts answers is a quiet nod to a predictive model. The luminous teal-to-indigo liquid and the glowing die make it feel like a lit display rather than a toy.

## aside-pane
- Idea: a small dark pane floating over a document page, a literal "aside" over your work: the question sits on top and the answer streams below.
- AI signal: the answer lines use a cyan-to-violet gradient, and the last one fades out mid-line as if it is still streaming.
