# Bolt as the stem

- `round-dot`: round "?" hook flows tangentially into a filled ⚡ (upper blade continues the stroke, step right, tapered blade) whose sharp tip aims at a round dot; indigo→cyan on cool white.
- `diamond-dot`: same hook-to-bolt hand-off, slightly steeper and wider bolt, with the dot made a sharp diamond to match the bolt; amber on dark navy.
