# Bolt + ask, figure/ground

- **strike**: a clean heavy "?" on the left, with a filled amber ⚡ overlapping its right shoulder. A transparent knockout gap around the bolt lets each symbol stay whole. Navy body. Menubar: the same two marks with less overlap and a 1.1 pt gap.
- **interlock**: the ⚡ stands in for the "?" stem. The hook passes behind the bolt through a knockout gap, and the bolt's tip points at the dot. Navy body. Menubar: a thinner hook and a larger bolt.

Knockouts are true gaps: an evenodd clipPath (canvas minus a round-joined offset of the bolt) clips the "?". Geometry is generated from a parametric "?" (concentric arcs) plus a 7-point bolt polygon.
