# Bolt-as-question-mark, angular

- `bolt-chevron`: the whole "?" is one filled lightning zigzag built only from 60° slants and horizontals (chevron hook, shelf step, tapered point) plus a 60° diamond dot; amber with light/shadow facet bevel on deep indigo.
- `bolt-octagon`: octagonal hook (45° facets, vertical left arm) flowing into a 60° lightning stem with shelf step and tapered point, diamond dot; indigo→cyan faceted bevel on cool white. Clearest "?" reading.
